Section opener — the alternative to wrapping everything in cards.

```jsx
<SectionHead label="Current epoch" title="Epoch 42" description="Eligibility freezes in 2 days." actions={<Button tone="ghost" size="sm">View evidence</Button>} />
```

Eyebrow is uppercase 12px; title 22px. The hairline is what separates sections — do not add a card around them.
